Adding a rota

In Chat, tap + and choose a file, a photo, or Scan a document. Your secretary finds your duties using the name you gave in Settings › "On rotas you're" (for example Sample, AS), then asks which calendar to use. If she can't find you, she'll ask what name you're listed under.

Checking if you're free

Ask in your own words: "Am I free 18–20 March?" or "I've been offered a list on 14 November". She checks every calendar, your rota and your Praxia Planner meetings, then offers to hold the dates or remind you to arrange cover.

Email on the Mac

Settings › Accounts. Automatic adds every account in Apple Mail; Manual lets you add them one by one. Draft replies appear in the Email section: press Copy reply, paste into your reply in Mail, and send it yourself.

Apple Intelligence

Summaries and drafts need Apple Intelligence switched on (iPhone 15 Pro or later, or a Mac with Apple silicon). Rotas, the diary and clash checks work without it.

Praxia Planner and Praxia Docs

Meetings and attachments from Praxia Planner appear in the Diary once Planner shares them. Scans and documents go to your secretary's Inbox in Praxia Docs on iCloud Drive, where she suggests where each one belongs.
