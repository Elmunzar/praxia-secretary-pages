Praxia Virtual Secretary

Your private medical secretary, on iPhone and Mac.

Name her, and she gets to work. She reads your rotas and adds your duties to your calendar, checks every calendar before you say yes to anything, keeps your meetings and their papers to hand, and on the Mac sorts your email, documents and payments.

Private by design. Reading, sorting and drafting are done by Apple's on-device intelligence. Your diary, mail and documents never leave your devices and your own iCloud. No account, no servers, no tracking.

What she does

  • Rotas: drop in a PDF, photo or scan; she finds your duties and adds them to the calendar you choose.
  • "Am I free?": she checks every calendar, your rota and your Praxia Planner meetings, and lists any clashes.
  • Diary: all your calendars and Planner meetings, with agendas and attachments.
  • Email (Mac): sorted into folders, summarised, with a draft reply for you to copy and send yourself.
  • Documents and money (Mac): filed where you approve; invoices, remittances and payslips matched up.
