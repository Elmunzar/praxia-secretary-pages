Privacy Policy

Praxia Virtual Secretary · iPhone and Mac

Last updated 28 October 2026.

In short: we don't collect any of your data. Everything the app does happens on your own devices and in your own iCloud.

What the app reads

With your permission, the app reads your calendars and reminders, the files you give it (rotas, documents, photos and scans), and on the Mac, the Apple Mail accounts you choose. It reads them only to do what you ask: show your diary, add rota duties, check for clashes, file documents, match payments, and draft email replies.

Where it's processed

All reading, summarising and drafting is done on your device by Apple's on-device intelligence. Nothing is sent to us or to any other company. The app has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Where it's stored

The app's settings and notes are stored on your device. Documents you choose to file go to folders on your Mac or in your own iCloud Drive (for example your Praxia Docs folders). Meetings shared with Praxia Planner are exchanged through your own iCloud. We cannot see any of it.

Email

On the Mac, if you switch email on, the app reads recent messages in the accounts you choose, sorts them into folders you approve, and writes draft replies for you to copy into Mail. It never sends email. Accounts you mark as clinical (for example NHSmail) are counted but never opened.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Your choices

You can withdraw calendar, reminders, files and Mail permissions at any time in System Settings or Settings on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its data on that device; files you filed remain in your own folders.
